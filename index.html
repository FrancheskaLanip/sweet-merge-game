Title: Apprentice

How to play

1. Aim and drop

Move your mouse or finger to aim, then tap or release to drop the sweet into the jar.

2. Merge pairs

When two matching sweets touch, they merge into the next sweet. Check the merge order under the board.

COMBO x3

3. Build combos

Merge again quickly to build a combo and multiply your points, up to x5.

4. Watch the line

If a sweet crosses the dashed line, the game is over. Bake the Birthday Cake for a big bonus!

Dango0 / 2

5. Bakery orders

Customers order sweets at the top. Merge to make the sweet they ask for before the timer runs out to get bonus points!

6. Specials and Fever

Golden Mochi counts double for orders. Bomb Candy explodes and pops nearby sweets. Fill the Fever bar with merges for 8 seconds of double points, and the line cannot end the game!

7. Power-ups

Score points to earn Swap, Shake and Pop. Swap changes your sweet, Shake wiggles the jar, and Pop removes one sweet. Keys 1, 2, 3.
